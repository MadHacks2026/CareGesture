# CareGesture

MadHacks 2026 | Qualcomm Track | Arduino UNO Q + Camera + Modulino Thermo
A privacy-conscious bedside communication prototype that translates simple hand gestures into caregiver requests using local Edge AI.

Some patients recovering from surgery or living with limited speech or mobility may struggle to speak or reach traditional call controls. CareGesture offers an additional communication channel for people who can reliably make small hand gestures. Simple, fatigue-free hand shapes are mapped directly to verified requests with two-way confirmation.

Our app uses a bedside camera feed watching a defined gesture area. The Qualcomm-powered UNO Q processes the video locally, confirms a recognized gesture, and creates a request. A nurse dashboard shows the request, allows acknowledgment and resolution, and returns the status to the patient.
